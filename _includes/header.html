Guild Wars 2 works amazingly well on SteamDeck. I've created simple and intuitive controls layout that should facilitate completely new players (SteamDeck owners looking for a new, free game to try) as well as experienced veterans (seasoned PC users open to play on a new device). Most important features of layout work out of the box, without any additional control modifications! That said, there are some optional features which can be enabled and icons to make it consistent with game UI.

I consider myself a rather casual but veteran player with over 12k hours played since game's release. Out of that, last 2k are played exclusively on Steam Deck with use of this layout. It's been personally tested by me in all game modes, including high end PvE with over 100 encounter clears per raid wing and all CM fractals.
